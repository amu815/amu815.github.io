# Conference badges

- Follow the research threshold and source limitations in README.md's Conference badge policy. Require explicit recognition by at least two distinct academic institutions, including at least one substantive evaluation, field survey, society governance document, institutional research report, or teaching document. At least one source must be independent of the conference organizer. Conference marketing, personal biographies, and institutional award/acceptance news alone are insufficient. Deduplicate copied claims and do not present this editorial classification as measured public opinion or universal certification.
- Keep field-leading conference status independent of CORE rank: B-ranked conferences can qualify. Record the bounded field, decision, review date, source URLs, document locations, and rationale in `content/conferences.ts` and the README before adding a classification. Preserve withheld decisions until sufficient new evidence supports a change.
- Reuse `conferenceSeries` when adding another year of a registered conference so its field-leading badge is applied automatically. Check new conference series against the same evidence-based policy; do not infer the badge from CORE rank or acceptance status.
- CORE A* uses the larger `core-a-star-badge` and never also receives the field-leading badge. This remains true if a registered conference changes rank.
- Recheck evidence when adding a new conference year, when contradictory evidence appears, and during annual site maintenance. Old sources can establish continuity but are not permanent authorization; record the new review date and substantive changes.
- Use `FieldLeadingBadge` consistently in publication, open-source research and timeline views. The badge links to the documented criteria and conference-specific evidence. Main-conference status does not extend to affiliated workshops.
- Reserve gold badges for awards and selections with financial support. Field-leading badges use the accent color; ordinary paper-type badges remain neutral.
