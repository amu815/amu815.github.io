# Conference badges

- Keep field-leading conference status independent of CORE rank: B-ranked conferences can qualify. Verify official conference or society descriptions and record the field and source in `content/conferences.ts` before adding a classification.
- Reuse `conferenceSeries` when adding another year of a registered conference so its field-leading badge is applied automatically. Check new conference series against the same evidence-based policy; do not infer the badge from CORE rank or acceptance status.
- CORE A* uses the larger `core-a-star-badge` and never also receives the field-leading badge. This remains true if a registered conference changes rank.
- Use `FieldLeadingBadge` consistently in publication, open-source research and timeline views. The badge links to its official source. Main-conference status does not extend to affiliated workshops.
- Reserve gold badges for awards and selections with financial support. Field-leading badges use the accent color; ordinary paper-type badges remain neutral.
